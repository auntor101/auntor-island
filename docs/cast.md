# Auntor Island: the cast

Everyone here is made up. No real people, places or events. Humour is PG-13 and aimed at ideas (franchises, councils, horoscopes), never at people.
Look notes: original anime designs, tall and good looking. Four base bodies are reused with different colours and props (man, woman, elf, wizard).

## The five regulars (village square)

**Nma** - Tall woman, long teal braid, sailor's coat, grin like she lost a bet and enjoyed it. Stands by the well in the market square.
Personality: dry, fast, always winning arguments nobody started.
- "{name}! You walk like someone with a plan. Do you have one?"
- "Sequel number nine. The hero is sad again. Shocking."
- "My horoscope says avoid conflict. So I'm only arguing a little."

**Ukk-ass** - Very tall man, silver undercut, long scarf, permanent smirk. Leans on the lantern post near the square.
Personality: smug, secretly kind, speaks in warnings that are mostly jokes.
- "Careful, {name}. The council is voting on air again."
- "Every film has a post-credit scene. Mine is me, leaving."
- "Fun fact: honey never goes off. Unlike my patience."

**Bang-odi** - Broad man, gold-brown hair, tiny round glasses, apron over a fancy waistcoat. Sits on the bench by the road to the Feast Quarter.
Personality: loud, warm, food-obsessed, takes everything personally in a funny way.
- "Hungry? Stupid question. Everyone is hungry. Go east."
- "A multiverse is just my fridge. Many options, nothing to eat."
- "You again, {name}! I knew it. The bench told me."

**Tom** - Lean man, messy dark hair, ink on his fingers, oversized notebook. Stands near the noticeboard in the square.
Personality: nerdy, over-explains, thrilled by facts, quietly proud.
- "Did you know lightning is five times hotter than the sun's surface?"
- "Moon's drifting away from us. Three centimetres a year. Rude."
- "I keep a list of every post-credit scene. It has opinions."

**Nanda** - Calm woman, lavender hair in a high bun, long jacket with star patches, wind chimes on her belt. Stands under the old tree at the edge of the square.
Personality: mystic roaster. Reads your sign, then reads you.
- "Born under a sign, {name}? Ah. That explains the shoes."
- "Mercury is in retrograde. Blame it for everything, including me."
- "The stars say you will walk somewhere. Brave stars."

## The Colonel

**The Colonel** - Enormous cape in sand and crimson, a tall feathered hat, waxed moustache, medals made of bottle caps. A fictional desert chieftain from nowhere in particular. Stands on the cart square beside the Great Hall now; his Garage opens later.
Personality: booming, vain, generous, narrates himself in third person, comic-strip strongman.
- "The Colonel has arrived! Applause is optional but encouraged."
- "My garage will hold forty dragons. Or one dragon and my opinions."
- "Have you met my cape, {name}? It has its own cape."

## Saga Theatre stage door (four invented film stars)

**Selkie Dune** - Tall woman, silver-blue bob, sparkling cloak, sunglasses at night. Leading lady of "The Long Tide".
- "Autographs cost one compliment. Make it good, {name}."
- "Premiere tonight. Yes, again. It is always tonight."
- "I only do my own stunts. The rest I do with dignity."

**Dax Orrel** - Tall man, swept auburn hair, leather coat, always posed for a camera that is not there. Action star, "Cliffhanger Jones".
- "I once jumped off a building. It was a short building."
- "Another sequel? Don't worry, I read the first one."
- "Post-credit scene in my life: me, still standing here."

**Pim Tarragon** - Slim man, curly green hair, big scarf, nervous energy. Comedy actor and secret poet.
- "Is that a fan? Or a very polite pigeon?"
- "My agent says be mysterious. So I won't tell you where I live. It's the green door."
- "Never trust a film with six heroes and one budget."

**Ravenna Quill** - Tall woman, violet-black waves, dramatic collar, tiny dagger-shaped earrings (props only). Villain specialist.
- "I play villains. In real life I recycle."
- "Evil laugh, one take. Want to hear it, {name}? No? Too late."
- "Heroes get the sequels. Villains get the good coat."

## Feast Quarter (shops, north row then south row)

**Tira Bellweather** - Round-cheeked woman, cream-gold hair, frilly apron. Behind the counters of the tiramisu and blueberry-cheesecake shops.
- "Coffee, cream, cocoa. Everything else is a rumour."
- "The cheesecake has blueberries. The blueberries have opinions."
- "One slice, {name}. Two, if you promise not to tell the diet."

**Mung-ma** - Short sturdy woman, black hair under a red headscarf, steam always rising. Cook at the Mungdi ramen-ish shop.
- "Noodles: long, hot, honest. Like me."
- "Slurping is a compliment. Louder is a bigger compliment."
- "Too spicy? That is the broth saying hello."

**Fenn** - Tall, thin barista, slicked mint-green hair, round shades, one very serious eyebrow. Runs the coffee shop.
- "Our coffee is a hug that wakes you up."
- "You look like a flat white in a latte world, {name}."
- "Sleep is a rumour spread by people who slept."

**Big Hob** - Big, bearded cook, tiny chef hat, muscles like loaves. Steakhouse grill.
- "Rare, medium, well. Or 'Hob's way,' which is perfect."
- "I sear it, I rest it, I name it. This one is Gerald."
- "Vegetables? We have a potato with ambitions."

**Sela** - Weathered, cheerful woman, sea-blue hair, captain's cap. Runs both the seafood and Asian-fusion counters ("fusion" = fish and rice, mostly).
- "Caught this morning. By a very confident man named Dev."
- "Fusion means I put things together and hope. It works."
- "Crab says hello. Mostly with his claws."

**Mr Plush** - Smooth tall man, velvet jacket, silver streak in dark hair, folded napkin on his arm. Host of the Velvet Lounge (a quiet, cosy lounge: armchairs, soft lamps, tea and music).
- "Welcome to the Velvet Lounge. Voices low, sofas high."
- "Our armchairs have seen things. All of them were naps."
- "May I take your coat, {name}? It looks tired."

## Villagers

**Granny Oat** - Small, sprightly grandmother, cloud-white hair, knitting bag, lilac shawl. Sits on a bench by the harbour road.
- "In my day we had one film. It was a boat. It was fine."
- "{name}, you're thin as a mast. Eat something."
- "The council wants a new bridge. They have not found the old river."

**Harbourmaster Brine** - Tall man, grey-blue coat, brass whistle, gull on shoulder (prop). Stands on the dock.
- "Ships come, ships go. Paperwork stays."
- "That gull is not mine. He just agrees with everything I say."
- "Wind from the west. Good for sailing and for gossip."

**Wick** - Slim young lamplighter, cap too big, ladder under one arm. Wanders the Feast Street, stands near its lantern posts at dusk.
- "I light eighty lamps a night. Sixty stay lit. That is a good score."
- "Dark is just light on holiday."
- "{name}! Hold my ladder. No, not like that. Wait."

## Banter categories (in src/story/cast.js)

Each person has 12 or more lines drawn from: day, night, snow, greeting by name, MCU rage bait (franchises, post-credit scenes, multiverses), astrology, science, island council politics (fictional council of Mayor Puddlewick), teasing, companion-aware, and one rumour hinting at a landmark (academy, arena, hatchery, springs, embers, icecave, tower, grove).

Content rules: PG-13, no gore, no slurs, no real names, no real countries or events, no sexual content. The Velvet Lounge is a cosy tea-and-music lounge, nothing more.
